Our Story

A local favourite, done properly.

Frick's is the kind of place that doesn't need much explaining. Fish that was still swimming this week, potatoes cut by hand each morning, and a batter that's been tweaked bite after bite until it's right.

Tucked on Queen Street in the heart of downtown Chatham, we're the first stop for families, hockey crowds, and anyone with a serious craving for the good stuff.
